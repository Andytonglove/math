# Active research plan — 8 October 2026

The user's approved project remains **Understanding AI-Generated Mathematical Proofs**. The current design supersedes the earlier emphasis on Graph construction and standalone model quiz accuracy.

Read these files first:
- `design_v1/MASTER_PLAN.md`
- `design_v1/MANUSCRIPT.md`
- `design_v1/PROTOCOL.md`
- `design_v1/status/SPRINT_STATE.json`

Primary object: a faithful, evidence-carrying, reusable mathematical understanding artifact. A producer reads an existing proof and freezes the artifact before realized test queries; a separate consumer uses the artifact without the original proof. Graph is optional. Human experiments are not required for initial machine-based claims; those claims do not establish human benefit.

The long-horizon work is a bounded series of follow-up research rounds, not a permanent computing process. The main automated-producer and held-out artifact-only consumer experiment remains unperformed. Preserve that limitation in every draft.

Keep the existing branch `research/proof-understanding-pilot` and do not alter main or original source mathematics. No paid API/GPU spending has been authorized. Each round must record actual evidence, code/tests, failures, costs and next scientific gate.
