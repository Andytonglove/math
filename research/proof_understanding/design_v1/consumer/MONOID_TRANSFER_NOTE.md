# A sharper 197 mechanism transfer: weaken GROUP to MONOID

**Status:** independent elementary mathematical argument inspired by source family's conditional operator representation; **not** a novel solution of Kaplansky's group-algebra conjecture.

## Mathematical change

The original local source uses a GROUP G. Its key group-algebra-to-configuration identity, however, requires only an associative multiplication with identity, finite support, and distributivity. We can ask what happens when G is replaced by a MONOID M.

Define two maps on natural numbers: L(n)=max(n-1,0) and R(n)=n+1, and let M be the monoid of transformations generated by L and R under composition. Write p=L and q=R, and multiply functions by composition: fg means f after g.

For every natural n, p(q(n))=L(n+1)=n, so pq=1 as elements of M. But qp is not 1 because q(p(0))=R(L(0))=1 whereas identity(0)=0.

Thus for ANY field K the distinct basis elements a=[p] and b=[q] of the monoid algebra K[M] satisfy ab=[1]=1 but ba=[qp] differs from 1. This is an elementary one-sided inverse in a MONOID algebra; replacing the word monoid by group here would be an invalid extension.

## Conditional cellular-type operator transport

For any finitely supported c in K[M], define T_c x(g)=sum_u c_u x(gu) for x:M->K. The same finite regrouping gives T_c T_d=T_cd. Therefore T_a T_b=1, so T_b is injective.

To show T_b is not surjective without relying on the abstract proof, observe: for every x, y=T_b x satisfies y(1)=x(q). Also y(qp)=x((qp)q)=x(q(pq))=x(q). Therefore y(1)=y(qp). But the indicator configuration delta_1 has delta_1(1)=1 and delta_1(qp)=0 because qp differs from 1. Consequently delta_1 is not in the range of T_b.

This is a complete elementary symbolic certificate, not just a finite simulation. Finite-prefix tests corroborate the shift identities but alone would not certify all natural numbers.
