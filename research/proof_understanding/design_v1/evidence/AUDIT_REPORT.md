## Invalid scientific inferences to avoid
(1) 23 public exercises do not equal 23 independent held-out proofs. (2) 100% deterministic oracle correctness is not LLM understanding accuracy. (3) Local calculation does not certify the advanced source. (4) A valid syntactic source anchor does not validate an induced dependency edge. (5) The absence of Graph gain in a 4-example-per-family Qwen pilot does not exclude its benefit on other models. (6) An artifact not frozen before novel queries cannot support source-removed causal transfer claims.
