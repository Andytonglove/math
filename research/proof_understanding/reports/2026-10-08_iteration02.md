## Concrete mathematical finding, carefully scoped

The parameter lemma requires \(\nu(A-\theta)>1-\theta\), while its other constraints imply \(A^2<\theta\). Then
\(1-\theta-2(A-\theta)=1-2A+\theta>(1-A)^2>0\). Therefore the *parameter lemma* cannot work at \(\nu\le2\). The strict boundary appears in the mechanism itself, not only in the theorem statement. This does NOT prove or disprove the stronger exponent-two approximation bound.

The collision argument has an independently computable finite combinatorial primitive: for n items in at most D types, the least possible number of same-type unordered pairs is attained by balanced occupancies.

## Quantitative caution

The witness choices become extreme near the boundary. With OUR PARTICULAR chosen rational parameters (not optimized), a floating-log estimate of the first dimension m satisfying just the sufficient condition cL>2 is about 5.3e9 at nu=2.001, 15,195 at nu=2.5, and 5,194 at nu=3. These are heuristic **diagnostics of one feasible construction**, not proof complexity lower bounds or full sufficiency checks. No theorem-strength inference can be drawn.
